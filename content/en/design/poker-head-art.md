The adopted art for all 41 Poker Head products. The selections were approved by the author and every PNG is exported; the delivery manifest records each file's source, author, size and hash.

## Visual language

Flat old-gold silhouettes on transparent backgrounds, recognisable at 32 pixels: no gradients, textures, shadows, glow or borders. Core, permanent, consumable and financial products share one colour; the interface frames and labels tell the categories apart. Card suits on icons are decorative and never restrict an effect to a suit. Spare Sleeve and Sleeve Clip deliberately share the card-exchange silhouette, so their category labels must stay visible.

## Selections

Thirty-six icons are recoloured Game-icons.net silhouettes (CC BY 3.0, credited in the game's `assets/CREDITS.md`). Five products had no suitable existing icon and received original code-drawn studies: Borrowed Glory, Shared Risk, Temporary Allowance, Aces to the Top and Fourfold Ticket. Their A, 21, +2 and x4 glyphs are deliberate identity marks drawn as vector paths, not UI text. The doc gives each product's subject and intended reading.

## Delivery

One 256x256 RGBA PNG per product in `assets/poker_head/`, trimmed, centred with a transparent margin and registered in the GUI asset manifest; the art masters and export plan stay in the separate art repository. Art never changes rules or effect text. The icons appear on [Items](items.html) and in the [Gallery](gallery.html); the earlier [candidate review](design-poker-head-icon-candidates.html) is historical.
