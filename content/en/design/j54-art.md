The 17 icons of J-54's v2 table (conditions, firmwares, operations and badges) are original code drawings from a deterministic script; no source images were processed.

Pixel lettering is J-54's mark, from the same pixel grid as its face and the Calculator Deck, so J-54's icons never share a colour group or letterform with Poker Head's smooth gold glyphs. Conditions are J-54's dark card stock with a red rim and a punch-card corner, a mark for what they read and their number below; firmwares use the same construction as a cartridge in the player's green; operations and badges are solid silhouettes, bold enough for the small chips in the right column. A few shapes avoid confusion: the Joker's jester hat hangs below its band so it does not read as Face Reset's crown, and Hot Purge uses a thermometer because the v1 Burn Protocol icon has a flame. The icons appear on [J-54's Table](table-j54.html) and in the [Gallery](gallery.html).
