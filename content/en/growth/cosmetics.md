title: Cosmetics
---
Looks change art only. They never change rules, probabilities or the run's balance fingerprint. Each player owns and equips looks separately.

There are decks (card back and optionally card face), tables, chip sets and avatars. Buy looks in Home's **Shop → Cosmetics**, grouped by series and slot; use **Profile → Wardrobe** to equip owned looks. Defaults are owned automatically; other looks are bought with chips (purchases count towards membership) or earned through achievements. The table lists shipped prices and unlock sources.

Poker Face unlocks Poker Head's whole set: his decks, table, chips and avatar. Those are cosmetic rewards; his playable Table is already available. Missing final art uses a procedural fallback; the [art gallery](gallery.html) labels installed files separately.
