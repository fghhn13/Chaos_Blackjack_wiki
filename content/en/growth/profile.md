---
title: Profile, wardrobe and saves
---
Click the player badge on Home to open **Profile**. **Overview** shows your avatar, tokens and lifetime record, and lets you rename the player. **Switch player** opens the player slots; each player's upgrades, looks and achievements are separate.

**Wardrobe** equips owned decks, tables, chip sets and avatars, with a live preview. A deck covers its card back and optionally its card face. Wardrobe shows owned looks; **More in the Shop** opens the shop to acquire new ones. **Achievements** shows progress and rewards, with hidden achievements concealed until unlocked.

Runs checkpoint at safe phases. **Continue run** restores the last checkpoint. Confirming abandonment records an abandoned run and pays no reward. Run results show newly unlocked achievements and allow rewarded looks to be equipped.

On phones, creating or renaming a player opens a native text field with composition input. Use Save or the keyboard's Done action; Cancel keeps the old name. Browser gameplay and account saves use the same player progression.
