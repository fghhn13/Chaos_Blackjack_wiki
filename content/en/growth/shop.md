title: Table shop and money
---
The table shop spends the run's cash; Home's [Shop](progression.html) spends chips from the vault. Shops open before betting, and purchases must leave enough cash for the minimum bet.

Poker Head's prices are written for a reference stake of {{ fact: settings.mode.chaos.money_base.value }} chips and scale with the chips you bring; the table below shows the reference values. It uses a limited permanent build and core-biased stock. See [builds, finance and replacement](poker-head-builds.html). Markup and discounts can change unpaid prices. Finance uses contract amounts; buyback refunds the actual price paid. J-54's Table sells nothing and shows no shop. Classic items retain bankroll-percentage prices.
