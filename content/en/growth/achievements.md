title: Achievements
---
Achievements are evaluated when a run settles. Most award chips to the vault; some also unlock cosmetics. Each unlock pays once. Abandoned runs do not contribute to lifetime stats or unlocks. Previously settled runs can be backfilled when the feature first reads an older player's history; missing counters contribute zero.

**Lifetime** goals accumulate over settled runs. **Single run** goals must be reached within one run. Rule Scholar counts different encountered Chaos rules. Rewards below come from the shipped balance profile.
