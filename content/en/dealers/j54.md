A calculating machine built as a circus clown: extravagant movement, pixel expressions and the voice of a compiler. It never touches your cards; it changes what its own cards are worth, one condition at a time, and somewhere in its deck it keeps a sealed sample it wants back. It hosts [J-54's Table](table-j54.html).
