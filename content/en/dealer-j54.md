**Theme: probability, calculation and deck reconstruction.**

J-54 is a mechanical computing device designed as a circus clown. Its theatrical movements and pixel expressions are exaggerated, yet it addresses the player in cold, precise programmatic language.

Instead of directly rewriting Blackjack's rules, it proposes to change the cards that make up the game itself. Its [Table design](table-j54.html) introduces duplication, removal, reordering, recycling and scanning, with some changes lasting across rounds.

The portrait and concept were supplied by the author. No playable mode, unlock condition or measured balance has been defined for this draft.
