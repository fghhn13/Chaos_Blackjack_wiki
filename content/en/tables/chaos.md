Choose a core, equip up to {{ fact: settings.mode.chaos.build_slots.value }} permanent products, and reach {{ fact: tables.chaos.target_money }} net assets. The [build guide](poker-head-builds.html) lists all cores, goods, consumables and financial products, with their actual effects and prices.

Poker Head announces the environment before shopping and betting; its persistent and timed rules can coexist. Read [environment mechanics and the offline dealer](poker-head-environment-plan.html). Cash, open principal and full loan repayment determine net assets; the minimum bet grows with rounds.

New runs have opening core choice and build state. Older saves without that state retain their earlier goal and behaviour. Build, Bag, Finance, Rules, Log and Run tabs show the run; victory rewards and cosmetics remain in their generated catalogs.
