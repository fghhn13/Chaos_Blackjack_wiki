Every card's value shifts by the same amount, Aces included (under −1 an Ace is worth 10 or 0). Negative shifts give you more room before busting, so totals that would normally be a stand can be worth a hit.

## Playing against it

- Re-read your total after the deal: the familiar two-card totals no longer apply.
- Original naturals settle before this rule. A shifted two-card total of 21 does not gain the natural payout.
- Doubling on a total that "looks like 11" is only right if the shown total really is 11.
